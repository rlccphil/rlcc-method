    Pillar I — REACH

    Reaching Everyone and Converting Hearts begins in your existing oikos.

    God has already placed every believer in a relational network—their oikos. REACH moves outward from intentional prayer and everyday love (GO), to spiritual conversations and the Gospel through the Real Life Bible Study (BRING), and finally into church belonging and water baptism (JOIN).

    Pillar II — CORE

    Creating Ongoing Relational Environments anchors believers in five settings.

    Conversion and church orientation are only the entry point. RLCC sustains spiritual growth through five ongoing relational environments—moving from corporate worship down to personal companionship—where members are cared for (CARE), grounded in godly habits (BUILD), and mobilized into active ministry (DEPLOY).

    Pillar III — LEAD

    Leadership Empowerment and Development multiplies healthy teams through readiness and credibility.

    RLCC does not hand out titles prematurely. Potential leaders are first spotted for READY character, trained through real tasks and God’s processing methods, assessed across five Credibility C’s before assignment into REACH, CORE, LEAD, or ADMIN, and ultimately coached to form and lead their own new community.
